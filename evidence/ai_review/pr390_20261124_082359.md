P1 — `scripts/m362_named_rotate.py:26-29,44-53`  
**The rotation script is not idempotent and can corrupt the archive.** After rotation, `NAMED.md` contains stubs such as `## M246 — VERBATIM...`, but `TARGETS` still contains `"## M246 "`. A second run’s `block_of()` will match the stub, treat the stub as the settled block, append the stub to `docs/named-archive-m362-rows.md`, and replace the archived block with the stub. P2 — `scripts/m362_sibling.py:235-260` (`writer_row`)  
**The writer direction census depends on a narrow role classifier and can silently undercount forms.** `writer_row` only recognizes index forms of the exact shape `lea reg,[base_reg+reg*2]`, then uses `roles_at()` to label operands as `p1`/`p2`. `roles_at()` returns `None` for definitions it does not understand, especially `lea`-defined registers, and those forms are excluded from `dirs` (`scripts/m362_sibling.py:257-259`). The claim “4 write `(p1,p2)+(p2,p1)`, 2 one-way” is therefore gated by a classifier whose failure mode can be hidden by the `None` filter rather than by a direct byte-level direction census.
